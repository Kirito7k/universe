Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Universe ("the app") is a personal note-taking app. This page explains what data the app uses and how.

Google account data

If you choose to turn on sync, the app asks Google for permission to access your Google Drive using the drive.file scope, plus your basic profile email (openid, email). With drive.file, the app can only see and change files that it created itself. It cannot read any other files in your Drive.

How the data is used

  • Your notes and learning progress are copied between your device and your own Google Drive so they stay the same on your computer and phone.
  • Your email address is shown in the app so you know which account is connected.

What the app does not do

  • There is no Universe server. Your data goes only between your device and Google.
  • The app does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.
  • The app does not use your data for advertising, analytics or training AI models.

Storage and deletion

The sign-in token is stored encrypted on your own device. You can sign out at any time in Settings → Sync, and remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's folder in your Google Drive removes the synced copies.

Universe's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
